Section 4

Required Course Reading Materials

Core readings, grouped by theme. Every entry links to its source.

Below are the required readings. The first four groups follow the weekly schedule in section 9 — one reading per discussion week, listed again on the day it is due. The last two groups are short and belong to specific projects rather than to a week, so you will not meet them in the weekly rhythm. Nothing here is optional reading you are quietly expected to have done. These materials are accessible via links using your institution login at no cost. Should you have issues accessing these materials, let me know when we do our syllabus review in WEEK ONE.
