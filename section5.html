Section 5

AI Bias Supplemental Readings

Fourteen case studies of algorithmic bias to journal and reflect on.

Below are 14 short AI bias case studies. This is a menu, not a queue: choose 4 across the semester to journal and reflect on in preparation for your short and final assignments. Pick the ones closest to your chosen identity markers, or to work you actually do. You are welcome to read more, but 4 is what is assigned. These readings will supplement the core readings materials in section 4. Your reflections and your argument must connect to the theories of intersectionality, feminism, and critical making.

HIRING & EMPLOYMENT ALGORITHMS
Amazon’s Biased Hiring Algorithm (2018) (gender bias)
“Why Amazon’s Automated Hiring Tool Discriminated Against Women.” ACLU, www.aclu.org/news/womens-rights/why-amazons-automated-hiring-tool-discriminated-against
Bloomberg’s ChatGPT/GPT hiring-bias test (2024)
Nicoletti, Leonardo, and Dina Bass. “OpenAI’s GPT is a Recruiter’s Dream Tool. Tests Show There’s Racial Bias”. Bloomberg, 2024, https://www.bloomberg.com/graphics/2024-openai-gpt-hiring-racial-discrimination/
CREDIT & LENDING ALGORITHMS
The Apple Card Credit-Limit Controversy (2019)
“Apple Card is Accused of Gender Bias. Here’s How That Can Happen.” CNN Business, 12 Nov. 2019, www.cnn.com/2019/11/12/business/apple-card-gender-bias
Racial Bias in Mortgage-Approval Algorithms (2021)
Martinez, Emmanuel, and Lauren Kirchner. “The Secret Bias Hidden in Mortgage-Approval Algorithms.” The Markup, 25 Aug. 2021, themarkup.org/denied/2021/08/25/the-secret-bias-hidden-in-mortgage-approval-algorithms
COMPUTER VISION/IMAGE GENERATION
The Google Photos “Gorilla” Mislabeling Incident (2015)
“Google Apologizes after App Mistakenly Labels Black People ‘Gorillas’.” CBC News, 2 July 2015, https://www.cbc.ca/news/trending/google-photos-black-people-gorillas-1.3135754
Twitter’s Racially Biased Image-Cropping Algorithm (2021)
Metz, Rachel. “Twitter Says Its Image-Cropping Algorithm Was Biased, So It’s Ditching It.” CNN Business, 19 May 2021, https://www.cnn.com/2021/05/19/tech/twitter-image-cropping-algorithm-bias/index.html
Bloomberg’s Stable Diffusion Investigation (2023)
Nicoletti, Leonardo, and Dina Bass. “Humans Are Based. Generative AI is Even Worse.” Bloomberg, 2023, https://www.bloomberg.com/graphics/2023-generative-ai-bias/
Google Gemini’s historically inaccurate image generation (2024)
Duffy Clare, and Catherine Thorbecke. “Google halts AI Tool’s Ability to Produce Images of People After Backlash.” CNN Business, 22 Feb. 2024, https://www.cnn.com/2024/02/22/tech/google-gemini-ai-image-generator
LANGUAGE & TRANSLATION ALGORITHMS
Gender Bias in Google Translate
Dino, Gino. “He Said, She Said: Addressing Gender in Neutral Machine Translation.” Slator, 22 Jan. 2019. https://slator.com/he-said-she-said-addressing-gender-in-neural-machine-translation/
AD TARGETING & DELIVERY ALGORITHM
Facebook’s Discriminatory Ad-Delivery Algorithm (2019)
“Facebook’s Ad-Serving Algorithm Discriminates by Gender and Race.” MIT Technology Review, 5 Apr. 2019. https://www.technologyreview.com/2019/04/05/1175/facebook-algorithm-discriminates-ai-bias/
GOVERNMENT ADMINISTRATIVE & BENEFITS ALGORITHMS
Michigan’s MIDAS Unemployment Fraud Algorithm (2013-2015)
“Michigan’s MiDAS Unemployment System: Algorithm Alchemy Created Lead, Not Gold.” IEEE Spectrum, https://spectrum.ieee.org/michigans-midas-unemployment-system-algorithm-alchemy-that-created-lead-not-gold
Idaho’s Secret Medicaid Budget Algorithm (K.W. v. Armstrong)
Stanley, Jay. “Pitfalls of Artificial Intelligence Decision making Highlighted in Idaho ACLU Case.” ACLU, 2 June 2017, https://www.aclu.org/news/privacy-technology/pitfalls-artificial-intelligence-decisionmaking-highlighted-idaho-aclu-case
HEALTHCARE ALGORITHMS
Racial Bias in a Healthcare Risk-Prediction Algorithm (Obermeyer et al., 2019)
“Millions of Black People Affected by Racial Bias in Health-Care Algorithms.” Nature, 2019, https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-019-03228-6

LLMs and race-based medicine (2023)
Omiye, Jesutofunmi A., et al. “Large Language Models Propagate Race-Based Medicine.” Npj Digital Medicine, vol. 6, 2023, article 195, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-023-00939-z
